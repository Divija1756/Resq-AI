The problem

Infrastructure fails exactly when it's needed most

Most disaster apps assume Internet → Server → User. RESQ-AI routes emergencies phone-to-phone when infrastructure fails — Person → Nearby Devices → Emergency Mesh → Rescue Team — so help doesn't wait for a signal bar.

System flow

Six steps from impact to rescue

01

Disaster alert triggers

Geo-fenced alert checks the user's last known coordinates against the affected radius and auto-arms emergency mode if they're inside it.

02

Are you safe?

A single tap — safe, need help, or critical — decides whether the flow stops here or escalates.

03

Emergency is classified

Manually selected, or typed in plain language and parsed by an offline text classifier for type, severity, people affected, and injuries.

04

Location is captured — no map, no internet

We read coordinates straight off the phone's GPS/GNSS chip. No Google Maps, no data connection — just a satellite fix, so it works exactly when the network doesn't.

05

SOS packet is sealed

Type, severity, headcount, injuries, coordinates and timestamp are packed into one structured record.

06

Send now, or queue and retry

With signal, it ships straight to command. Without it, it's held locally and retried the moment any channel — cellular, mesh, or satellite — reappears.
